# [[balaji's newsletter]]

[Link to newsletter -->](https://balajis.com/)

## Structure

Balaji uses his newsletter as distillation layer for ideas previously shared and sharpened through conversations in forums like X and YouTube. These forums act as a feedback loop wherein he can share half-baked ideas, gather comments, reactions, and feedback, and then use the higher fidelity format of a Substack newsletter to act as a showcase for more refined thinking and insights. In addition to the flywheel effect this workflow creates, the ability to use forums like X to have multiple lines in the water simultaneously (e.g., multiple posts generated comments and engagement from a broad array of people) serves as an always-on catalyst for lateral thinking, in turn generating more interesting and fresher thinking/ideas continuously, reducing the 0-1 effort often required when creating new knowledge products where more generic thinking often occurs.
