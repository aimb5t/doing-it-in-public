This [quartz vault](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) is an [open-source](https://opensource.com/resources/what-open-source) collection of [[information assets]] comprised of raw materials, organized knowledge, and finished products. I am actively adding to, learning though, and building from it. 

I see the curation of this knowledge base as an [inductive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_reasoning) exercise in building: 
-  a mirror to hone my own perspective and taste
- a pillar of a public identity
- a information pipeline or [[personal 'back-end']] system
- a repository for more fluid expression / sharing and use of ideas
- a dedicated space to centralize learning and clarify thinking

Ideally, each a entry has the attributes of a what I consider to be a strong knowledge asset (not notes for notes' sake):
- they are high quality principles or units of knowledge to build up from with the potential to generate or catalyze interesting insights and [[credible points of view]] 
	- like citations used in a research paper, or books selected or inclusion in a reference library
- they have been tested / validated / refined in through conversation / engagement / practice / reflection and iteration over time, whenever possible
	- like [[balaji's newsletter]] workflow and structure 
- they act as an immediately useful accelerant/reminder for the completion a given task, serve to flatten the learning curve on a given topic
	- like learning uploads in the matrix![[Pasted image 20250905235751.png]]

Building information assets is part of a larger concept I am thinking through around [[asset-based frameworks for work or labor exchange]].

I welcome constructive feedback and conversations. [Reach out here](mailto:aimeecburnett@gmail.com).
